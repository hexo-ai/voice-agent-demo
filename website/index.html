Most agents expect you to sit at a screen.

Agents can now write code, run research and handle customers. But you reach most of them through a text box, and they answer with a wall of text. Voice agents are the exception, and each one takes weeks to build. People have more than eyes. We talk, we listen, we take calls on the move.

Agents work for hours. You can't sit and watch.

A coding agent or a research agent can run on its own for hours. When it needs a decision, it sends a notification and waits until you're back at your desk.

Reading text aloud isn't a conversation.

A real conversation means short answers instead of long text. Asking before acting. Calling you back when there's news. Sending the links and details by text.

Building for voice is slow.

Patients, customers and field teams still call. Voice agents can take those calls, but building and testing each one takes weeks of back-and-forth between an expert and an engineer.

SIA sits in the middle.

On one side it speaks the agent's language. On the other, plain conversation. It listens, keeps answers short, asks before the agent acts, calls back when there's news or when the agent is stuck, and texts the details. Your agent stays unchanged.

Use case 01SIA Build

Build a voice agent just by talking.

For: teams that build voice agents, and the domain experts who know what each agent should do.

The problem

A voice agent demo takes an afternoon. Getting it into production takes weeks.

The domain expert knows the rules. The engineer builds the agent. They go back and forth, test after test, for every new agent. Growth is capped by how many engineers you can hire.

How SIA Build helps

The expert talks to SIA Build. It asks the questions an engineer would ask, builds the agent, tests it on simulated calls, and improves it. The expert only reviews the hardest calls. No engineer in the loop. Our goal: hours, not weeks.

Use case 02SIA Risk

Find the risks in your voice agent before your callers do.

For: teams that ship voice agents and test them before every launch and every prompt change.

The problem

The risky failures hide in calls nobody thought to write down. A caller changes an answer, mentions a symptom late, or pushes back.

Good human testers find these, but it takes weeks per agent. Scripted test lists follow the easy path.

How SIA Risk helps

  • You give it your agent's endpoint and its prompt.
  • It calls your agent hundreds of times, like a rigorous tester. Its search is rewarded for one thing: finding risk. So it digs wherever the agent slips.
  • It asks your expert when it's unsure whether something is a risk.
  • You get a risk report: every risk, its severity, and the calls that prove it.
  • It suggests fixes: prompt edits, or fine-tuning when a prompt isn't enough. Then it checks again after every change.

Use case 03SIA Phone

Give your agents the missing piece: a phone.

For: anyone who gets work done with AI agents.

The problem

With people, you pick how to talk: email, chat, a video call, or a quick phone call. With your agents, you get one way in: a screen you have to read.

Step away and the work stops. Questions and updates pile up as notifications you'd have to stop, open and read.

How SIA Phone helps

  • One phone number for all your agents. Call it from a walk, between meetings, wherever you are.
  • Ask "what's waiting for me?" It picks the right agent for each request and answers in a few sentences.
  • It asks before it acts, then does what you say.
  • It calls you back when an agent is done or needs you, and texts you the links.
Use case 04SIA Phone API

Give your agent a phone number.

For: teams that build text agents: support, sales, internal assistants.

The problem

Most agents are text only. Your users are often not at a screen: driving to a customer, on a job site, between meetings.

Building a separate voice agent means a second product to build, test and keep in sync.

How SIA Phone API helps

  • Add about three lines of code. Your agent gets a real voice and a phone number.
  • Same agent, same tools, same data. Nothing to rebuild.
  • It answers calls in a few clear sentences and asks before it acts.
  • It calls back with results and texts the charts and links.

Research

We research how people and agents should work together, and build what we learn. Our first published work is SIA, a method for agents that improve themselves, accepted at ICML. It's how SIA Build and SIA Risk fix agents, with prompt changes and fine-tuning.
